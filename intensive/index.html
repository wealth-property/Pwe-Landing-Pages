Strategic Role
What job this acquisition performs inside the architecture
"A property can look like a good deal — and still be the wrong deal for the wealth architecture you are building."
Most property decisions are evaluated in isolation. The purchase price looks acceptable. The yield seems reasonable. The location feels right. And on those terms, the acquisition may genuinely be a good deal.
But the question the Intensive teaches you to ask first is not whether the deal is good. It is whether the deal serves the objective.
"What job does this asset perform inside my overall wealth architecture?" — That question changes what you look for, what you decline and what you move on.
